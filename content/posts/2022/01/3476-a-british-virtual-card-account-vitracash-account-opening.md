# 一款英国虚拟卡账户 VitraCash开户教程

> 本文同步自 [jiami.dog 官方原文](https://jiami.dog/3476.html)，以官网版本为准。

## 注册要求以及说明

注册需要英国手机号、护照/身份证

英国帐户虚拟卡及实体卡BIN为53736500~53736506，德国账户为53736507~53736514，其他请查询网站，是由Monavate Limited发行的万事达[借记卡](https://jiami.dog/tag/debit-card "借记卡")~

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/4382dbae3717c0a7d2c13b87a4a5ff93.png?ssl=1)

这可不是预付卡

## 网上申请过程

### 1.打开官网，点击Request Access

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/eb96168b5f8d240cf23ee041299ac98f.png?ssl=1)

### 2.输入邮箱，然后点击Get Invite

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/52a292b08bee40f1b0575139314e5b48.png?ssl=1)

### 3.打开Google Play/App Store下载Vitra App

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/d42ff0c1746a3a4744a1e64df903b682.png?ssl=1)

### 4.点击Continue

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/80cb51c870f0841bc1f63810b9815b54.jpg?ssl=1)

### 5.输入手机号，然后点击Continue

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/40954332fd254ea9cebe57112b2fcab1.jpg?ssl=1)

### 6.输入简讯认证码

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/ab935968b6e1e82a02cad6d26783a39e.jpg?ssl=1)

### 7.输入姓(Last Name)、名(First Name)、邮箱以及生日，设置密码后点击Sign up

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/2523ace601a2a599734161daa2d58f1f.jpg?ssl=1)

### 8.选择英国(United Kingdom)后点击Continue

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/b02d169470af729ae35c1e45b76576f3.jpg?ssl=1)

### 9.点击Start

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/34b69c503f0c939b8fb7c725811069f4.jpg?ssl=1)

### 10.勾选协议后点击Continue

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/e152259643eaa720c48514b1e157c246.jpg?ssl=1)

### 11.点击Continue后根据提示完成自拍

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/9bcd6607744aba7665f1f6c8ee025583.jpg?ssl=1)

### 12.选择护照发行国后点击Continue

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/9a398b1767e78aea7a46378df2176652.jpg?ssl=1)

### 13.直接点击Continue，根据提示拍摄护照

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/db96b72237fa3188d04f6cc6639f8151.jpg?ssl=1)

### 14.直接点击Confirm

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/dcea6cb82129383c47230dfac64bcd19.jpg?ssl=1)

### 15.点击Finish等待审核即可

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/04900d154d206e1a0d2b7f61b7978408.jpg?ssl=1)

### 16.这时回到App已经可以看到卡号等信息了

![](https://i0.wp.com/jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/68de4d0b72193aa5fad60ddf74eabe5b.jpg?ssl=1)

实体卡长这样~
