# 温德姆酒店集团夏天送福利：住二送一

> 本文同步自 [jiami.dog 官方原文](https://jiami.dog/1987.html)，以官网版本为准。

![温德姆酒店集团](https://jiami.dog/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/1622899483-wyndham-brands.jpg)

[温德姆](https://jiami.dog/tag/wyndham "温德姆")在其会员计划下推出了一个全新促销活动，这个活动和我们之前常见的酒店住二送一类似，但实际上区别在于，给的奖赏并非房券，而是7500的温德姆积分。

活动详细地址：[**https://www.wyndhamhotels.com/wyndham-rewards/hotel-deals/stay-two-nights**](https://www.wyndhamhotels.com/wyndham-rewards/hotel-deals/stay-two-nights)

Book direct by September 2 and complete your qualified stay by September 6, 2021. Free night redemption varies by location, ranging from 7,500–30,000 points per bedroom.

注册参与此次促销活动并在 2020 年 9 月 2 日前在直销渠道预订客房；在 2020 年 9 月 6 日前完成符合资格的住宿。请确保在完成符合资格的住宿前注册参与本促销。

积分兑换免费住宿视各酒店不同，每晚住宿需要 7,500点–30,000 点积分不等。

> **Stay 2 Nights, Get 6,500 Bonus Points Terms & Conditions:**
>
> Capitalized terms used without definition in these [offer](https://jiami.dog/tag/offer "offer") terms and conditions (“Offer Terms”) will have the meanings ascribed to them in the Wyndham Rewards program terms and conditions, available at <http://www.WyndhamRewards.com/terms> (the “Program Terms”). As more particularly set forth below, this promotion (“Offer”) offers Members the opportunity to receive 6,500 Wyndham Rewards bonus points (the “Bonus”) for up to three (3) Offer Qualifying Stays (as defined below). The Bonus, when added to the minimum of 1,000 Base Points a points-earning Member earns for a Qualified Stay pursuant to the Program Terms, results in earnings of at least 7,500 Wyndham Rewards points per Offer Qualifying Stay. 7,500 Wyndham Rewards points are good for 1 standard bedroom per night at thousands of Participating Hotels, subject to availability. At most properties, a Go Free award applies to room rate only, including taxes. See the Program Terms for more information about how to redeem Wyndham Rewards points.

A Member who is eligible to receive a Bonus pursuant to these Offer Terms will typically receive it within two to four weeks after the completion of the applicable Offer Qualifying Stay.

积分两到四周后入账。

An “Offer Qualifying Stay”). Maximum of three (3) Bonuses per Member.

每个会员仅限最多参与三次这个活动。
